About the Project
Turning Algebra Into Action
Solar Smart Home demonstrates how algebra helps households make informed decisions about solar energy. Users enter their daily electricity consumption or appliance usage, and the system calculates the number of solar panels required, the estimated electricity cost, the annual savings, and the environmental benefits — turning a confusing decision into a simple, data-backed one.
Enter your usage → Get the exact number of panels, the cost, the savings, and the planet's benefit — instantly.

The Math Behind It
The Algebra That Powers the System
N = E  (Daily Energy in kWh) P (Panel Output in kW) × S (Sunlight Hours)
Rounded up to the next whole panel
E

Daily Energy ConsumptionTotal kWh used by the household per day

P

Panel OutputRated wattage of one solar panel, in kW

S

Sunlight HoursAverage peak sunlight hours per day

N

Panels RequiredN = E ÷ (P × S), rounded up
